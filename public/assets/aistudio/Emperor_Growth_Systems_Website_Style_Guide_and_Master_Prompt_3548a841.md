# EMPEROR GROWTH SYSTEMS

## Website Style Guide & Master Build Prompt

**Royal Luxury • Modern Business Systems • International Audience**

**Purpose:** A reusable design and build reference for creating a
polished, conversion-aware website that reflects the Emperor Growth
Systems identity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Brand foundation

Emperor Growth Systems is a general business-systems brand serving an
international audience. Its website should communicate practical
expertise in organizing business operations, improving lead management,
strengthening customer follow-up, and implementing useful CRM and
workflow solutions.

### Brand personality

-   Premium and confident, without appearing arrogant or inaccessible.
-   Modern and intelligent, with clear information architecture.
-   Trustworthy and outcome-oriented rather than tool-focused.
-   Growth-minded and versatile across industries.
-   Elegant and distinctive, but never flashy, cluttered, or excessively
    decorative.

### Core positioning

We help businesses organize the way they capture, manage, and follow up
with opportunities through practical CRM systems, workflow improvements,
and automation.

### Brand promise

Make business operations clearer, customer follow-up more consistent,
and growth processes easier to manage.

### Recommended colour balance

Use this as a starting point, not a rigid formula:

-   40% Champagne Ivory --- keeps pages open, readable and calm.
-   25% Midnight Forest --- provides depth, authority and structure.
-   15% Royal Emerald --- reinforces the brand identity.
-   15% Gold --- makes the brand recognizable and adds emphasis.
-   5% Obsidian Charcoal --- supports text and contrast.

### Colour usage rules

-   Use gold as an accent and signature, not as the background for every
    section.
-   Use Midnight Forest for navigation, footer, hero sections and
    high-contrast blocks.
-   Use Champagne Ivory for most content backgrounds.
-   Use dark green or charcoal for paragraph text; do not use bright
    gold for small body copy.
-   Avoid placing gold text on pale cream where contrast is weak.
-   Keep gradients selective: one prominent metallic treatment per
    visual section is usually enough.

## 5. Website template description

**Recommended template:** a premium, editorial-style business services
website with a strong conversion path. Use generous whitespace, large
typography, restrained gold details, structured service cards, and clear
calls to action. The site should feel like a specialist business
consultancy and systems studio---not an agricultural website, generic
SaaS dashboard, or over-decorated luxury fashion site.

### A. Header / navigation

Logo on the left; navigation links: Home, Services, How It Works, About,
Insights, Contact. One gold-accented CTA such as "Book a Discovery Call"
or "Discuss Your Business". Use a Midnight Forest or ivory header; keep
it clean and sticky only if it improves usability.

### B. Hero section

Two-column layout on desktop: left side has a clear outcome-led
headline, short supporting paragraph and two CTAs; right side has a
refined abstract workflow/CRM visual or a simple process illustration.
Use a Midnight Forest background with restrained metallic gold details,
or an ivory background with a dark-green headline.

### C. Problem / value proposition

Explain common operational friction: missed inquiries, inconsistent
follow-up, scattered customer information, manual repetitive tasks and
unclear pipelines. Keep language empathetic and specific.

### D. Services

Use 3--4 cards with concise outcomes:

-   CRM Setup & Optimization
-   Lead Management & Follow-up Systems
-   Workflow Automation
-   Business Process Improvement

Explain who each service helps and what practical improvement it
supports.

### E. How it works

Show a simple 3- or 4-step process: **Understand the workflow → Map the
system → Build and configure → Test, hand over and improve.** Use
numbered steps, thin gold connectors and clear explanations.

### F. Why work with us

Focus on clarity, practical implementation, thoughtful configuration,
communication and systems built around the client's workflow. Do not
claim years of experience, client numbers, testimonials, certifications
or measured results unless verified.

### G. Featured insight / resources

Show selected articles, checklists or practical guides. Use editorial
card layouts and clear reading links. This section supports trust and
organic discovery.

### H. Final CTA

Use a dark emerald or Midnight Forest band with one concise invitation,
a short reassurance statement and a clear contact action.

### I. Footer

Include logo, concise brand statement, key links, contact details,
social links and copyright. Use Midnight Forest with ivory text and
small gold accents.

## 6. Reusable component and layout rules

-   **Buttons:** primary gold or metallic-gold treatment with dark text;
    secondary emerald with ivory text; tertiary text links with clear
    hover states.
-   **Cards:** ivory or white surfaces, subtle borders, restrained
    shadows and consistent corner radius. Avoid heavy glassmorphism.
-   **Icons:** simple line icons in emerald or gold; use one consistent
    icon family.
-   **Borders and dividers:** thin gold or pale neutral lines, used
    sparingly.
-   **Photography and illustrations:** authentic business, people,
    workflows and technology contexts; avoid cliché gold coins, crowns
    everywhere, or agricultural imagery unless the page topic is
    agriculture.
-   **Spacing:** generous whitespace, consistent section padding and
    readable line lengths.
-   **Responsive behaviour:** mobile-first layouts, stacked hero
    sections, comfortable touch targets and no horizontal overflow.
-   **Accessibility:** semantic headings, keyboard focus states,
    descriptive alt text, readable contrast and clearly labelled forms.
-   **Motion:** subtle transitions only; respect reduced-motion
    preferences and avoid distracting animations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Conversion, content and trust requirements

-   Every major page should have one clear primary action.
-   Use outcome-led copy: explain what becomes easier or more organized
    for the client.
-   Avoid feature dumping and vague claims such as "revolutionize your
    business".
-   Use truthful proof only. If testimonials or case studies are not yet
    available, use a process explanation, sample workflow or clearly
    labelled demonstration instead.
-   Contact forms should ask only for information needed to start a
    conversation and should show a clear success state.
-   Provide clear privacy and data-handling information where forms
    collect personal information.
-   Make email and phone contact details configurable; never invent
    business contact details.
-   Ensure all links, forms, buttons and mobile navigation work before
    launch.

## 8. Master website-build prompt

Copy the prompt below into your AI website builder or coding assistant.
Replace bracketed placeholders with your actual business details.

### ROLE

Act as a senior brand designer, UX/UI designer, conversion-focused
website strategist, and experienced frontend developer.

### PROJECT

Design and build a polished, responsive website for Emperor Growth
Systems, a general business-systems brand serving an international
audience.

### BUSINESS POSITIONING

Emperor Growth Systems helps businesses organize the way they capture,
manage, and follow up with opportunities through practical CRM systems,
workflow improvements, and automation. Communicate business outcomes
rather than simply listing software tools. The site should appeal to
small and growing businesses, service providers, consultants, agencies,
startups, and entrepreneurs across multiple industries.

### PRIMARY WEBSITE GOALS

1.  Explain the business clearly and credibly.
2.  Present services in terms of problems solved and practical outcomes.
3.  Build trust without inventing client results, testimonials,
    credentials, or experience.
4.  Encourage qualified visitors to start a conversation or request a
    discovery call.
5.  Provide a foundation for publishing useful business insights and
    resources.

### VISUAL IDENTITY --- ROYAL LUXURY

The design must feel premium, modern, authoritative, trustworthy,
intelligent, and growth-oriented. It must not look like an
agricultural-only brand, a generic technology template, a fashion house,
or an excessively flashy luxury site.

Use these exact brand colours:

-   Imperial Gold: `#D4AF37`
-   Golden Radiance: `#FFD700`
-   Royal Emerald: `#075B3D`
-   Midnight Forest: `#092C24`
-   Champagne Ivory: `#FFF9ED`
-   Obsidian Charcoal: `#202522`

Use Imperial Gold as the signature accent. Use metallic gold gradients
selectively for logo treatments, key highlights, dividers, and selected
calls to action. The primary metallic gradient is:

``` css
linear-gradient(110deg, #8A6414 0%, #B8860B 15%, #D4AF37 30%, #FFF2AE 43%, #FFD700 52%, #C99519 68%, #FFF0A0 82%, #9B741B 100%)
```

Use Midnight Forest for depth and authority, Royal Emerald for
supporting brand elements, Champagne Ivory for spacious backgrounds, and
Obsidian Charcoal for readable text. Do not use bright gold for small
paragraph text. Avoid excessive gradients and maintain strong contrast.

### TYPOGRAPHY

-   Playfair Display for hero headlines and major editorial headings.
-   Montserrat for navigation, body text, labels, cards, buttons, forms,
    and interface elements.

Use a clear typographic hierarchy, comfortable line-height, restrained
letter spacing, and responsive sizing.

### DESIGN DIRECTION

Create an editorial-style premium business services website. Use
generous whitespace, strong alignment, clean grids, elegant typography,
restrained gold detailing, simple line icons, subtle borders, and
polished but understated interactions. Prefer a refined mix of ivory
content sections and deep-green feature sections. Use real business
context imagery or purposeful workflow illustrations. Do not fill the
interface with decorative crowns, coins, random gradients, or stock
imagery that does not explain the service.

### SITE STRUCTURE

1.  **Header:** logo, Home, Services, How It Works, About, Insights,
    Contact, plus a prominent "Discuss Your Business" or "Book a
    Discovery Call" CTA.
2.  **Hero:** outcome-led headline, short explanation, primary CTA and
    secondary CTA. Include a tasteful visual showing organized leads,
    customer follow-up, or connected business workflows.
3.  **Business challenges:** explain missed inquiries, inconsistent
    follow-up, scattered customer records, repetitive manual tasks, and
    unclear sales pipelines.
4.  **Services:** CRM Setup & Optimization; Lead Management & Follow-up
    Systems; Workflow Automation; Business Process Improvement. For
    each, state the audience, problem addressed, deliverables, and
    expected practical benefit without guaranteeing results.
5.  **How It Works:** Understand the workflow → Map the system → Build
    and configure → Test, hand over and improve.
6.  **Why Emperor Growth Systems:** emphasize practical thinking, clear
    communication, thoughtful configuration, and solutions shaped around
    the client's process.
7.  **Insights/resources:** space for articles, checklists, guides, and
    educational content.
8.  **Final CTA:** concise invitation to discuss a business process or
    operational challenge.
9.  **Footer:** brand statement, navigation, configurable contact
    information, social links, and legal links.

### COPYWRITING

Write concise, confident, plain-English copy for an international
business audience. Lead with customer problems and business outcomes,
not software names. Avoid exaggerated promises, fake statistics,
fabricated case studies, invented client logos, invented testimonials,
and unsupported claims. Use clear calls to action and explain what
happens after a visitor submits a form.

### FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

-   Fully responsive on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
-   Accessible semantic HTML and heading order.
-   Keyboard-accessible navigation, buttons and forms.
-   Visible focus states and readable colour contrast.
-   Working navigation, CTAs, contact form validation, loading state,
    and success/error feedback.
-   Reusable components and consistent design tokens.
-   SEO-ready page title, meta description, social sharing metadata,
    descriptive image alt text, and clean URL structure.
-   Good performance: optimized images, minimal unnecessary
    dependencies, and no heavy animations.
-   Use placeholders for unknown contact details and clearly identify
    them for replacement.
-   Do not pretend a form sends messages unless the backend/email
    integration is actually configured and tested.

### DELIVERABLE

Produce a complete, polished website with maintainable code and reusable
components. Before finishing, check the mobile layout, contrast,
typography, navigation, links, form behaviour, and consistency with the
brand palette. Explain any integrations or credentials still needed
rather than claiming they are configured.

## 9. Page-specific template copy prompts

### Homepage

Write a concise homepage for Emperor Growth Systems. Start with a clear
business outcome, explain the operational problems addressed, introduce
the four service areas, show the implementation process, and finish with
a direct invitation to discuss the visitor's workflow. Keep the tone
premium, clear, and credible.

### Services page

For each service, describe who it is for, the business problem, what is
included, how implementation works, what the client needs to provide,
and the practical outcome. Avoid guarantees and unsupported performance
claims.

### About page

Explain the brand's philosophy: practical systems should make business
operations clearer and customer follow-up more consistent. Focus on
approach, values, working principles, and commitment to clarity. Do not
invent founder history or credentials.

### Contact page

Create a welcoming contact page with a short explanation of the next
step, a concise inquiry form, clear field labels, privacy reassurance,
and honest success/error states. Keep contact details as editable
placeholders until verified.

### Insights page

Create an editorial resource library for articles about CRM, lead
management, customer follow-up, workflow design, automation, and
business operations. Use clear categories, readable cards, and
descriptive article titles.
